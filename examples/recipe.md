# Weeknight chickpea curry

A one-pot dinner ready in about thirty minutes.

## At a glance

::: kv
- **Prep**: 10 minutes
- **Cook**: 20 minutes
- **Serves**: 4
- **Difficulty**: easy
:::

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons coconut oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 can chickpeas, drained
- 400 g crushed tomatoes
- 2 teaspoons curry powder
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Method

::: steps
1. Heat the oil in a wide pot over medium heat.
2. Cook the onion until soft, then add the garlic.
3. Stir in the curry powder for thirty seconds.
4. Add the chickpeas and tomatoes; simmer for twenty minutes.
5. Season, then serve with rice or flatbread.
:::

## Notes

::: note
The curry keeps for three days in the fridge and freezes well.
:::

::: success
Pairs well with a crisp green salad and a squeeze of lime.
:::
