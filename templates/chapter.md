# The lighthouse

The fog came in before the tide did, rolling over the harbor in
low gray waves.

## The keeper

Mara had kept the light for eleven years. She knew every stair,
every draft, and every creak of the lamp mechanism.

> A lighthouse does not chase the ships. It simply stays lit.

## The letter

The letter arrived on a Tuesday, folded inside a parcel of books.
It was addressed to the previous keeper, who had left no address.

For a moment the wind, the lamp, and the sea were quiet. Then Mara
read the first line and began the climb.
